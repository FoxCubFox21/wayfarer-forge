What's on it

Things to actually do

Not a stock world with a permissions plugin bolted on. These are custom systems, written and debugged one at a time.

⚔️

Crystal duels — against something that learns you

1v1 in a protected arena with a full netherite kit. Nobody else queued? A practice bot fills the slot, pillars after you, pearls, eats gapples and pops totems — and after every fight it re-tunes its own difficulty toward an even match. Your gear is stashed and handed back whatever happens. /duel

🎁

Seventeen crates

One per shulker colour, Starter up to Immortal, each with its own loot table and the win odds printed on every prize. Free daily and vote keys. /crates

🏪

Nine NPC shops

Mining, redstone, building, farming, storage, combat, brewing, food and curiosities — real currency, real prices, no chest shops to hunt for.

🔍

Scavenger hunt

Eight critters hidden around spawn — a frog, a dolphin, a bee and five more. Find them all for a one-of-a-kind trophy. /hunt progress

📊

Hall of Records

A floating monument of counter-rotating rings that shows your stats in mid-air — or anyone else's, if you ask for them by name. /hof

🌍

Into the wild

Walk through the portal at spawn and land somewhere random within 50,000 blocks. Claim it, build on it, keep it. /rtp

🛡️

Genuinely protected

Land claims, rollback logging on every block placed or broken, anti-xray, seed protection and a real anticheat. Grief gets undone, not argued about.
