House rules

Short list

01

Claim your base

Unclaimed land is fair game — anyone can build on it, break it, or empty your chests. Claiming is free: drop a chest for an automatic starter claim, or use a golden shovel. You get 2,500 blocks to start and can hold as many claims as you like.

02

Grief inside a claim gets undone

Break into someone's claim and it gets rolled back and you get removed. Outside claims we don't referee — that is exactly what claiming is for.

03

The End is contested ground

Claims work in the overworld and the Nether. They do not work in the End — anything you leave there can be taken or broken by anyone. End cities are meant to be raced for.

04

PvP is on everywhere

Not just the duel arena — the open world too. Carry what you can afford to lose, and don't expect protection out in the wild.

05

No cheating

X-ray, reach, kill aura, auto-clickers. The anticheat catches most of it; the block logs catch the rest.

06

Be decent in chat

Disagree all you like. Slurs, harassment and spam are where it ends.

07

Don't lag the server

Giant mob farms and redstone that tanks the tick rate will get trimmed. Ask first if you're unsure.

08

One account, no alt abuse

Alts to farm daily keys or dodge a ban aren't on.

Questions

Before you ask

Do I need mods or a launcher?

No. Vanilla Minecraft, no mods, no client install. Add the address and play.

Can I play on Bedrock, phone or console?

Yes. Geyser and Floodgate are running, so Bedrock players join the same world as Java players. Use play.bedrock.wayfarerforge.games on port 19132. You don't need a Java account.

What version do I need?

The server runs Java Edition 26.2. ViaVersion is installed, so a good range of older Java clients can connect too — if yours doesn't work, update to 26.2.

Does it cost anything?

No. There's no store, no ranks for sale and nothing to buy. Everything on this page is free to everyone.

Is my base safe if I log off?

Only if you claim it. Claimed land can't be broken, opened or built on by anyone you haven't trusted, whether you're online or not. Unclaimed land has no protection. Claims work in the overworld and the Nether, but not in the End — don't leave anything you care about out there.

What happens to my gear in a duel?

It's stashed before the match and handed straight back afterwards — win, lose, disconnect or server restart. You fight with a supplied netherite kit, not your own stuff.

The bot is too easy or too hard.

It re-tunes itself after every fight to land near an even match. Give it a few duels and it will meet you where you are.

How many people can be on at once?

A hundred slots. It's a small server and it's usually quiet — that's rather the point.
