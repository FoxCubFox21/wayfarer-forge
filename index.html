What's on it

Things to actually do

Not a stock survival world with a permissions plugin bolted on — these are custom systems, built and debugged one at a time.

⚔️

Crystal duels

1v1 in a protected arena with a full netherite kit. No one around? A practice bot fills the queue — and it tunes itself to your skill so it stays roughly an even fight. /duel

🎁

17 crates

One for every shulker colour, Starter up to Immortal, each with its own loot table and the win odds shown before you open it. Free daily and vote keys. /crates

🏪

Nine shops

Mining, redstone, building, farming, storage, combat, brewing, food and curiosities — staffed by NPCs and paid for in server currency.

🔍

Scavenger hunt

Eight critters hidden around spawn — a frog, a dolphin, a bee and five more. Find them all for a one-of-a-kind trophy. /hunt progress

📊

Hall of Records

A floating monument of orbiting rings that shows your stats in mid-air — and anyone else's if you ask for them. /hof

🌍

The wild

Step through the portal at spawn and land somewhere random within 50,000 blocks. Claim it, build on it, keep it. /rtp

🛡️

Actually protected

Land claims, rollback logging on every block, anti-xray, seed protection and a real anticheat. Grief gets undone, not argued about.

🌆

Eternal golden hour

Spawn, the shop and the arena sit in permanent twilight under clear skies — while it rains and cycles normally everywhere else.

Getting in

How to join

  1. Open Minecraft: Java Edition on version 26.2.
  2. Go to Multiplayer → Add Server.
  3. Paste play.wayfarerforge.games as the address and save.
  4. Join, then type /help — everything's listed in there.
